What Events processes

Events processes account and sign-in details; adult and Scout profiles; troop membership and requests; event details, locations, rosters and attendance; health and safety entries; emergency and vehicle contacts; push tokens and notification content; limited operational errors; and, when billing is enabled, subscription and purchase status. Apple or Google handles raw payment credentials.

Why and with whom

Data is used to authenticate users, coordinate troops and events, provide authorized safety information, deliver notifications, support subscriptions, secure the service and answer requests. Supabase is the principal backend. Apple, Google, Expo and RevenueCat process limited information for selected sign-in, platform, push or subscription functions. Authorized profile controllers and event managers can receive information for the requested workflow.

Events does not sell personal or health data. The audited app contains no advertising, behavioral-analytics, session-recording or dedicated crash-reporting SDK.

Children and sensitive data

The current app has no age gate or guardian verification. Attendance permission is not a distinct, versioned privacy consent for health-data collection or sharing. These are launch blockers, not claims of compliance. Read the Children's & Parent Privacy Notice and Health & Safety Data Notice.

Retention, security and choices

No general automated retention schedule was verified. Records may remain until an authorized deletion, account deletion cascade, administrator action or separate maintenance process. Verified safeguards include authenticated access, row-level controls, server authorization, HTTPS transport, protected native session storage and secret separation. Backup windows and vendor settings still need confirmation.

What Events processes

Events processes account and sign-in information; adult and Scout profiles; troop membership and requests; event details, locations, rosters and attendance; emergency contacts; health, safety and transportation entries; push tokens and notification content; limited operational errors; support communications; and, when native billing is configured, subscription and purchase information.

Health and safety auto-save

The event safety form automatically saves reusable medical, allergy, accommodation, emergency-contact, phone, email and transportation fields after the user types and when the app backgrounds or the user exits. This can happen before Submit and, for a Scout profile, before the attendance-permission checkbox is selected. The saved profile may prefill later event forms. The attendance checkbox is not a separate, versioned privacy consent or guardian-authority record.

RevenueCat and store purchases

For a signed-in native user, Events uses the Supabase account UUID as the RevenueCat App User ID and requests offering and subscription state. If the user purchases or restores, RevenueCat and Apple or Google process the receipt and purchase history, product, store, transaction identifiers, localized price and period, entitlement, purchase date, expiration, renewal, cancellation, billing issue, refund or revocation, environment and management information. Apple or Google handles raw payment credentials.

The audited app code does not send RevenueCat the user's email, name, phone number, health or safety fields, or custom customer attributes. The production Supabase purchase-sync migration and RevenueCat Edge Functions were still not deployed when rechecked July 31, 2026, so paid access is not production-ready.

Why and with whom

Data is used to authenticate users, coordinate troops and events, provide authorized safety information, deliver notifications, administer purchases, secure the service and answer requests. Supabase is the principal backend. Apple, Google, Expo and RevenueCat process limited information for selected identity, platform, push or subscription functions. Authorized profile controllers and event managers can receive information for the requested workflow.

Events does not sell personal or health data. The audited app contains no advertising, behavioral-analytics, session-recording or dedicated crash-reporting SDK.

Children, authority and consent

The current app has no age gate, adult attestation, guardian verification or verifiable parental-consent record. These are launch blockers, not claims of compliance. Read the Children's & Parent Privacy Notice and Health & Safety Data Notice.

Retention, deletion and choices

No general automated retention schedule was verified. Records may remain until an authorized deletion, account-deletion cascade, administrator action or separate maintenance process. In-app account deletion does not automatically delete the RevenueCat customer or independently controlled store transaction records. Backup windows, processor retention and deletion procedures still need confirmation.

Email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion. Do not email passwords, medical details or payment credentials. We may verify identity and authority.

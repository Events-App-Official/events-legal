What Events processes

Events processes account and sign-in information; adult and Scout profiles; troop membership and requests; event details, locations, rosters and attendance; emergency contacts; health, safety and transportation entries; push tokens and notification content; limited operational errors; support communications; and, when enabled, subscription and purchase information.

Eligibility, authority and safety consent

Accounts are limited to adults age 18 or older by self-attestation. Events does not collect date of birth or independently verify age, parenthood, guardianship or other authority. A Scout manager records an authority attestation, but that attestation is not independently verified.

Health and safety information is not remotely saved merely while it is typed, when the app backgrounds or when the user exits. Before a remote safety write, the user must affirm health-and-safety consent, emergency-contact authority and, for a Scout, the applicable authority relationship, then submit the form. The user selects either event-only consent or reusable-profile consent. Active consent can be withdrawn; withdrawal and any required retention handling are subject to the product controls and applicable obligations.

RevenueCat and store purchases

For a signed-in native user, Events uses the Supabase account UUID as the RevenueCat App User ID and requests offering and subscription state. If the user purchases or restores, RevenueCat and Apple process the receipt and purchase history, product, store, transaction identifiers, localized price and period, entitlement, purchase date, expiration, renewal, cancellation, billing issue, refund or revocation, environment and management information. Apple handles raw payment credentials.

The app does not send RevenueCat the user’s email, name, phone number, health or safety fields, or custom customer attributes. Events does not sell personal or health data and the audited source contains no advertising, behavioral-analytics, session-recording or dedicated crash-reporting SDK.

Why and with whom

Data is used to authenticate users, coordinate troops and events, provide authorized safety information, deliver notifications, administer enabled purchases, secure the service and answer requests. Supabase is the principal backend. Apple, Google, Expo and RevenueCat process limited information for selected identity, platform, push or subscription functions. Authorized profile controllers and designated event safety managers can receive information needed for an approved workflow.

Retention, deletion and choices

The configured safety-data defaults are 60 days for event safety submissions, 30 days for transportation data, 365 days before reusable-profile reconfirmation and a 30-day reusable-profile grace period. A retention worker must be separately scheduled and its destructive runs separately authorized; configured defaults do not prove that every record has already been deleted on that schedule.

In an account-deletion operation, Events attempts RevenueCat subscriber deletion before authentication-user deletion. Store transaction records remain independently controlled by Apple and may be retained for restoration, fraud, accounting, tax, dispute, legal or platform obligations. Email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com to request access, correction, consent withdrawal or deletion. Do not email passwords, medical details or payment credentials.

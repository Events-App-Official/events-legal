Privacy Policy

Effective August 23, 2026 · version privacy-2026-08-23

What Events processes

Events processes account and sign-in information; adult and Scout profiles; troop membership and requests; event details, user-entered venue locations, rosters and attendance; emergency contacts; health, safety and transportation entries; push tokens and notification content; limited operational errors; support communications; and historical payment records for accounts that previously used paid infrastructure.

Eligibility, authority and safety consent

Accounts are limited to adults age 18 or older by self-attestation. Events does not collect date of birth or independently verify age, parenthood, guardianship or other authority. A Scout manager records an authority attestation, but that attestation is not independently verified.

Health and safety information is not remotely saved merely while it is typed, when the app backgrounds or when the user exits. Before a remote safety write, the user must affirm health-and-safety consent, emergency-contact authority and, for a Scout, the applicable authority relationship, then submit the form. The user selects either event-only consent or reusable-profile consent. Active consent can be withdrawn; withdrawal and any required retention handling are subject to the product controls and applicable obligations.

Payments in this release

The current app release is free. Its normal client path does not initialize RevenueCat, request offerings or customer subscription state, make or restore purchases, or open subscription management. Dormant paid infrastructure and historical payment records may be retained for security, legal, accounting or dispute handling. New free accounts do not require a RevenueCat customer.

Events does not sell personal or health data and the audited source contains no advertising, behavioral-analytics, session-recording or dedicated crash-reporting SDK.

Why and with whom

Data is used to authenticate users, coordinate troops and events, provide authorized safety information, deliver notifications, secure the service and answer requests. Supabase is the principal backend. Apple or Google may process selected sign-in information. Expo and the Apple or Google push services process device registration and notification delivery information. Authorized profile controllers, troop administrators, event creators and designated event safety managers can receive information permitted by the applicable workflow.

Retention, deletion and choices

The configured safety-data defaults are 60 days for event safety submissions, 30 days for transportation data, 365 days before reusable-profile reconfirmation and a 30-day reusable-profile grace period. A retention worker must be separately scheduled and its destructive runs separately authorized; configured defaults do not prove that every record has already been deleted on that schedule.

For a free account with no recorded RevenueCat history, account deletion does not depend on RevenueCat availability or configuration. An account with historical paid records may require separate processor cleanup, while store transaction records remain independently controlled by Apple or Google. Email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com to request access, correction, consent withdrawal or deletion. Do not email passwords, medical details or payment credentials.

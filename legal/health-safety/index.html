Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice

Effective August 23, 2026 · consent version health-safety-2026-08-23

Events collects health data when an adult participant or authorized adult profile manager supplies medication or food needs, allergies and reactions, accommodations, or other health and safety notes. These details come from the adult who completes the form or from a reusable profile saved by an authorized adult. Events uses this data to prepare event safety forms, prefill future forms when requested, and help designated adults coordinate participant safety.

Consent before remote saving

Events 2.0 requests affirmative consent before saving health data. The safety form explains the collection purpose, available scopes, and recipients. Consent to collection and consent to sharing use separate confirmations. Providing information for a Scout also requires confirmation of your authority to act for that Scout.

Purpose and access

Health notes, medication or food needs, allergies and reactions, and accommodations in an event submission may be shared with authorized adult profile managers, the event creator, and designated adult event safety managers. Authorized adult profile managers may access reusable health information. Supabase processes the data for Events as its hosted backend provider. Events does not share consumer health data with affiliates, sell it, or use it for advertising.

Consent, withdrawal and retention

Withdraw active collection and sharing consent in Settings → Safety Forms → Withdraw safety consent, or contact us below. Withdrawal restricts future use and sharing and queues affected records for deletion. Event safety submissions become eligible for deletion 60 days after the event. Reusable profile consent requires reconfirmation after 365 days.

Your choices

To confirm whether Events collects, shares, or sells your health data, access or correct it, obtain the applicable list of recipients and their contact information, withdraw consent, or request deletion, email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com without including health information. We may verify your identity and authority. If we decline your request, reply to our decision to appeal. Washington consumers may contact the Washington Attorney General if an appeal is denied.

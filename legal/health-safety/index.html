Health & Safety Data Notice

Publication draft — separate health-data collection and sharing requirements need attorney review and product implementation.

Last updated July 31, 2026 · health-v3-publication-draft

Events may process medication or food needs, allergies and reactions, special needs, participant, parent and emergency-contact details, attendance permission, driving information, vehicle make/model, license plate, available seats and event-specific copies.

Automatic reusable-profile saving

The current event form writes reusable safety-profile fields to Supabase after the user types and when the app backgrounds or the user exits. This can happen before Submit and, for a Scout profile, before the attendance-permission checkbox is selected. The reusable profile may prefill a later event form.

Purpose and access

Data is used to prefill a safety profile, create an event submission and show relevant information to the controlling account and authorized managers for event coordination. Supabase is the principal backend processor. Events does not sell this data or use it for advertising.

Important limits

Events is not a health-care provider, medical record, emergency service or medical device. It does not verify information, guarantee manager review or contact emergency personnel. Call 911 or the applicable emergency number in an emergency.

Consent and retention reality

The Scout checkbox records permission to attend; it is not a separate collection-consent version, authority attestation, administrator-sharing choice, selected-field record, recipient list or withdrawal record. The operator must either stop pre-consent saving or implement the counsel-approved collection, sharing and withdrawal controls required for the intended launch states.

No general automated retention period was verified. Do not upload official medical records, diagnoses, insurance details, Social Security numbers or full medical histories.

Your choices

For access, correction, withdrawal or deletion questions, email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com without including sensitive details. Verification may be required.

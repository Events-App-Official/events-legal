Health & Safety Data Notice

Publication draft. Implemented collection consent and role-scoped sharing still require attorney review; the product does not offer a separate field-by-field recipient selection.

Last updated August 8, 2026.

Events may process medication or food needs, allergies and reactions, special needs, participant, parent and emergency-contact details, attendance permission, driving information, vehicle make/model, license plate, available seats and event-specific copies.

Consent before remote saving

The current event form does not remotely write reusable safety-profile fields after typing, on backgrounding or on exit. Before a remote safety write, the user must affirm health-and-safety consent and emergency-contact authority, choose event-only or reusable-profile scope, and submit. Scout information also requires a current Scout-authority attestation.

Purpose and access

Data is used to maintain a consented reusable profile, create an event submission and show relevant information to the controlling account and authorized event safety managers for event coordination. Supabase is the principal backend processor. Events does not sell this data or use it for advertising.

Important limits

Events is not a health-care provider, medical record, emergency service or medical device. It does not verify information, guarantee manager review or contact emergency personnel. Call 911 or the applicable emergency number in an emergency.

Consent, withdrawal and retention

Attendance permission is separate from health-and-safety consent, emergency-contact authority and Scout-authority attestations. Active safety consent can be withdrawn. The configured defaults are 60 days for event safety submissions, 30 days for transportation data, 365 days before reusable-profile reconfirmation and a 30-day reusable-profile grace period. A retention worker must be separately scheduled and destructive runs separately authorized.

Do not upload official medical records, diagnoses, insurance details, Social Security numbers or full medical histories.

Your choices

For access, correction, consent withdrawal or deletion questions, email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com without including sensitive details. Verification may be required.

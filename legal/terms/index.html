Service, eligibility and authority

Events is a troop and event coordination tool. Accounts are limited to adults age 18 or older through an affirmative self-attestation. Events does not collect date of birth or independently verify age, identity, parenthood, guardianship or organizational authority. Before entering another person’s information, a user must have authority and provide only relevant, accurate information.

Scout management requires an affirmative parent, legal-guardian, organization-authorized-adult or other-authorized-adult attestation. That record is not independent verification of the claimed authority.

Safety limits

Events is not a health-care provider, medical record, emergency dispatch service, background-check service or substitute for professional judgment. In an emergency call 911 or the applicable emergency service. Users remain responsible for original forms, medications, supervision and organizational procedures.

Acceptable use

Users may not violate law or another person’s rights; submit information without authority; exploit, harass or endanger anyone; disclose private roster, contact, location or safety data outside the intended group; bypass access controls; upload malicious content; or imply an affiliation or endorsement they do not have.

Subscriptions

Only iOS subscription capability is currently enabled. Available store offerings may include monthly, quarterly and yearly Troop Pro subscriptions; no free trial is offered, and one active subscription covers one troop. The store provides the price and renewal period before purchase. Android and web subscriptions remain disabled. Database and deployed Edge Function product allowlists were aligned on August 8, 2026; App Store, RevenueCat dashboard and signed-device lifecycle results remain separate launch gates.

Unresolved contract terms

No arbitration, class-action waiver, governing-law clause or venue is included in this public draft. Warranty, liability, administrator indemnity, termination and dispute terms require licensed counsel and operator approval before final acceptance is requested.

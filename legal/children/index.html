Children's & Parent Privacy

Publication draft — not a claim of COPPA or state-law compliance.

Last updated August 8, 2026.

Scout profiles may include names, troop and event participation, contact information, attendance, emergency contacts, allergies or safety needs, and transportation information.

Current age and authority controls

After authentication-account creation, Events requires a self-attestation that the user is age 18 or older before protected account and profile actions can continue. It does not collect date of birth or independently verify age, parent or guardian identity, or legal authority. Scout management requires a recorded parent, legal-guardian, organization-authorized-adult or other-authorized-adult attestation; that is an attestation, not independent verification or verifiable parental consent.

Safety information and consent

Health, safety, emergency-contact and transportation information is not remotely saved while typed, on backgrounding or on exit. Before a remote safety write, the user must affirm health-and-safety consent and emergency-contact authority, select event-only or reusable-profile scope, and submit. Attendance permission remains separate from privacy, health-data consent and authority attestations.

Intended use

Scout information is intended to be supplied by a parent, guardian or another authorized adult. It is used for troop, event and safety coordination. Authorized event safety managers and service providers receive data needed for that workflow. Events does not sell children's information and the audited source contains no ads or behavioral analytics.

Parent requests

Email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com to ask for review, correction, consent withdrawal or deletion, or to report unauthorized child access. Do not send detailed medical information or identity documents in ordinary email. Identity and authority may need verification.

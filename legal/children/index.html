Children's & Parent Privacy

Publication draft — not a claim of COPPA or state-law compliance.

Last updated July 31, 2026 · children-v2-publication-draft

Scout profiles may include names, troop and event participation, contact information, attendance, emergency contacts, allergies or safety needs, and transportation information.

Current age and authority limitation

The current app does not collect age or date of birth, verify that an account holder is an adult, verify parent or guardian authority, or record verifiable parental consent. Profile setup requires an adult name and a Scout name, but those labels do not prove identity, age or authority. The operator must choose and implement an age and authority model before launch.

Safety information is saved before the checkbox

The event safety form auto-saves reusable medical, allergy, accommodation, emergency-contact, phone, email and transportation fields while they are typed and when the app backgrounds or the user exits. For a Scout profile, this can occur before the attendance-permission checkbox is selected. That checkbox is not verifiable parental consent and is not a versioned privacy consent or authority record.

Intended use

Scout information is intended to be supplied by a parent, guardian, the Scout where legally permitted, or another authorized adult. It is used for troop, event and safety coordination. Authorized event managers and service providers receive data needed for that workflow. Events does not sell children's information and the audited source contains no ads or behavioral analytics.

Parent requests

Email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com to ask for review, correction or deletion, or to report unauthorized child access. Do not send detailed medical information or identity documents in ordinary email. Identity and authority may need verification.

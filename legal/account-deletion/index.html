Account Deletion

This page explains deletion options; it does not submit a deletion request.

Effective and last updated August 23, 2026.

Delete in the app

Sign in, open Settings → Delete Account, review the warning, reauthenticate if requested and type DELETE. A troop owner must first transfer or delete owned troops.

Get signed-out help

Email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com with “Account deletion” in the subject. Do not send a password, authentication code, identity document, payment details or health information. Events may verify the account and requester's authority.

What may remain

Account-only data is intended to be removed through the deployed deletion process. Shared records, another person's independently controlled information, legally required records and historical references may require limited retention or de-identification. Already-issued authentication tokens may remain valid until expiry even after the authentication user is deleted.

Historical payment records

The current release is free. Accounts with no recorded RevenueCat history can be deleted without a RevenueCat key, customer or network response. If an account has recorded historical paid activity, the deletion process retains the existing processor-cleanup safeguard. Apple or Google transaction records remain independently controlled and may be retained for restoration, fraud prevention, accounting, tax, dispute, legal or platform obligations.

Timing

No universal active-system, processor or backup deletion period is published. The configured safety-data defaults are 60 days for event safety submissions, 30 days for transportation data, 365 days before reusable-profile reconfirmation and a 30-day reusable-profile grace period. A retention worker must be separately scheduled and destructive runs separately authorized.

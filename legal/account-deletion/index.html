Account Deletion

Publication draft — this page is informational and does not submit a deletion request.

Delete in the app

Sign in, open Settings → Delete Account, review the warning, reauthenticate if requested and type DELETE. A troop owner must first transfer or delete owned troops. An external renewing subscription may need to be canceled through Apple or Google.

Get signed-out help

Email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com with “Account deletion” in the subject. Do not send a password, authentication code, identity document or health information. Events may verify the account and requester's authority.

What may remain

Account-only data is intended to be removed through the deletion process. Shared records, another person's independently controlled information, legally required records and historical references may require limited retention or de-identification. Store subscriptions are controlled separately. Already-issued authentication tokens may remain valid until expiry even after the authentication user is deleted.

Timing

No universal active-system, processor or backup deletion period is currently verified. Events will not publish a specific deadline until its technical controls, vendor settings and legal obligations are confirmed.

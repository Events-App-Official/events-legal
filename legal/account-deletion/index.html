Account Deletion

Publication draft — this page is informational and does not submit a deletion request.

Last updated July 31, 2026 · deletion-v3-publication-draft

Delete in the app

Sign in, open Settings → Delete Account, review the warning, reauthenticate if requested and type DELETE. A troop owner must first transfer or delete owned troops. An external renewing subscription may need to be canceled through Apple or Google.

Get signed-out help

Email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com with “Account deletion” in the subject. Do not send a password, authentication code, identity document, payment details or health information. Events may verify the account and requester's authority.

What may remain

Account-only data is intended to be removed through the deployed deletion process. Shared records, another person's independently controlled information, legally required records and historical references may require limited retention or de-identification. Store subscriptions are controlled separately. Already-issued authentication tokens may remain valid until expiry even after the authentication user is deleted.

RevenueCat and store records

The current in-app deletion function does not automatically delete the RevenueCat customer or send deletion work to RevenueCat, Apple or Google. Purchase and transaction records may remain for subscription restoration, fraud prevention, accounting, tax, dispute, legal or platform obligations. Use the email above for a separate processor-deletion request; Events may be unable to erase records controlled independently by a store.

Timing

No universal active-system, processor or backup deletion period is currently verified. Events will not publish a specific deadline until its technical controls, vendor settings and legal obligations are confirmed.

The Supabase migration and Edge Functions needed to synchronize and verify RevenueCat purchases remained absent when rechecked July 31, 2026.

What RevenueCat processes

For a signed-in native user, Events sends the Supabase account UUID to RevenueCat as the App User ID and requests the configured offering and subscription state. If the user purchases or restores, RevenueCat and Apple or Google process the receipt and purchase history, product, store, transaction identifiers, localized price and period, entitlement, purchase date, expiration, renewal, cancellation, billing issue, refund or revocation, environment and subscription-management information.

The audited app code does not send RevenueCat the user's email, name, phone number, health or safety information, or custom customer attributes. Apple or Google collects raw payment credentials; Events does not receive raw card or bank details.

Renewal and cancellation

A store subscription renews until canceled through Apple or Google. Cancellation stops a future renewal and normally leaves access through the verified paid-through date. Deleting the app, troop or Events account does not itself cancel an external store subscription.

Refunds

Canceling and requesting a refund are different actions. Apple controls refunds for Apple purchases and Google Play controls refunds for Google Play purchases, subject to applicable law. Events cannot guarantee or override the store's decision. A verified refund, reversal or revocation may end paid access.

Deletion

In-app Events account deletion does not automatically delete the RevenueCat customer or independently controlled Apple or Google transaction records. A separate verified processor-deletion request may be required, and some transaction records may be retained for restoration, fraud, accounting, tax, dispute, legal or platform obligations.

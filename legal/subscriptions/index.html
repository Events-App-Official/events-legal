Subscriptions & Refunds

Publication draft — billing is not production-ready and no purchase should be offered from this draft.

The current source is designed for one monthly Troop Pro subscription. The purchase screen must show the current localized Apple or Google price and renewal period. No free trial is verified. The live backend required to synchronize and verify purchases was absent when checked on July 30, 2026.

Renewal and cancellation

A store subscription renews until canceled through Apple or Google. Cancellation stops a future renewal and normally leaves access through the verified paid-through date. Deleting the app, troop or Events account does not itself cancel an external store subscription.

Refunds

Canceling and requesting a refund are different actions. Apple controls refunds for Apple purchases and Google Play controls refunds for Google Play purchases, subject to applicable law. Events cannot guarantee or override the store's decision. A verified refund, reversal or revocation may end paid access.

Payment information

Apple or Google collects raw payment credentials. Events and RevenueCat receive limited purchase, product, store, transaction, renewal/expiration and entitlement status.

Subscription questions: eventsofficialapp@gmail.com. Do not email payment credentials or full receipts.

Subscriptions & Refunds

Publication draft. iOS subscription capability is enabled and the deployed product allowlists are aligned, but App Store, RevenueCat dashboard and signed-device lifecycle verification remain required before a paid-launch readiness claim.

Last updated August 8, 2026.

Available iOS store offerings may include monthly, quarterly and yearly Troop Pro subscriptions. The purchase screen obtains the localized Apple price and renewal period from the store. No free trial is offered, and one active subscription covers one troop. Android and web subscriptions remain disabled.

What RevenueCat processes

For a signed-in native user, Events sends the Supabase account UUID to RevenueCat as the App User ID and requests the configured offering and subscription state. If the user purchases or restores, RevenueCat and Apple process the receipt and purchase history, product, store, transaction identifiers, localized price and period, entitlement, purchase date, expiration, renewal, cancellation, billing issue, refund or revocation, environment and subscription-management information.

The app does not send RevenueCat the user's email, name, phone number, health or safety information, or custom customer attributes. Apple collects raw payment credentials; Events does not receive raw card or bank details.

Renewal and cancellation

An iOS store subscription renews until canceled through Apple. Cancellation stops a future renewal and normally leaves access through the verified paid-through date. Deleting the app, troop or Events account does not itself cancel an external store subscription.

Refunds

Canceling and requesting a refund are different actions. Apple controls refunds for Apple purchases, subject to applicable law. Events cannot guarantee or override the store's decision. A verified refund, reversal or revocation may end paid access.

Deletion

In an account-deletion operation, Events attempts RevenueCat subscriber deletion before authentication-user deletion. Apple transaction records remain independently controlled and may be retained for restoration, fraud, accounting, tax, dispute, legal or platform obligations.

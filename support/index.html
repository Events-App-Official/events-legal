Support & Contact

Last updated August 23, 2026.

Email eventsofficialapp@gmail.com for account/app support; privacy, correction, consent-withdrawal or deletion requests; parent or guardian questions; health-data questions; or suspected security problems.

Protect sensitive information

Use a descriptive subject and the account email when appropriate. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment credentials, government identity documents or detailed medical information by ordinary email. Events may ask you to verify identity or authority through a safer channel.

This site has no support form, account lookup, tracking, cookies or analytics. Selecting the email link opens your email application; sending is your action.
